# codebreaker

## :jigsaw: Overview
This app helps people who design escape rooms, scavenger hunts, or puzzle games bring their challenges into a digital format. The puzzles and clues still happen in real life, but players use the app to enter codes, scan QR clues, and unlock the next stage of the game.

In short: the fun happens in person, and the app acts as the **digital game master** that keeps track of puzzles, progress, and multiplayer updates.

## :female_detective: Motivation
- Explore my interest in escape rooms by designing puzzle mechanics digitally.
- Strengthen my skills in mobile development, backend integration, and real-time updates.
- Create a foundation that _could_ scale into a SaaS-style platform in the (far) future. 

## :rocket: Features (MVP scope)
- **Two user types:**
    - Room Designers/Admins - create puzzles and organise rooms.
    - Players - join rooms and solve puzzles.
- **Puzzle components:** basic inputs, number locks, image maps.
- **Room flow:** puzzles linked to QR codes or numeric identifiers.
- **Multiplayer sync:** puzzle unlocks and timeouts are updated across all players.
